Common Questions

Everything you might be wondering.

How long does a detail take?
An Express wash or interior refresh takes about an hour. Full Exterior and Full Interior take roughly 2 hours each, and Wash & Wax is about 3 hours. The Clay & Seal runs around 4 hours, and the full Signature Showroom Detail takes 5–6 hours. Trucks, vans and 3-row SUVs may run slightly longer and carry a small large-vehicle surcharge — I'll confirm everything when you book.
Do you come to me, or do I drop the car off?
I come to you. As long as you're within ~10 miles of Atherton (94027), there's no travel fee. I just need access to a hose and an outdoor outlet. Outside the radius? Reach out — happy to talk.
What if I'm not happy with the result?
You don't pay until you've inspected the car and given me the thumbs-up. If anything's off, I'll fix it on the spot, free. Your satisfaction is the only thing I'm trying to earn.
How do I pay?
Cash, Venmo, Zelle, or Apple Pay. No card fees, no surprises. Payment happens after the job, in person.
Can you get pet hair / heavy stains out?
Almost always, yes. Pet hair removal is a $29.99 add-on and heavy stain removal is $14.99 per seat or carpet area. Trim restoration is $49.99 if your plastics are looking faded. Showroom Club members get a free pet-hair or odor add-on each visit. For severe cases, send a photo when you book and I'll quote it honestly.
What are your hours?
I'm available 7 days a week, 7:30 AM – 4:00 PM. Pick whatever day fits your schedule — weekday lunch break, Saturday morning, Sunday afternoon, it's all open. Need an off-hours slot? Text me at (650) 400-7254.
